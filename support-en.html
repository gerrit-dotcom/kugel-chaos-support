How to play — Version 3.0

Save Saturn: drag the planet into the golden target ring without touching the cat. You can also use directional controls and “Secure”. Three safe routes complete the adventure.

Comet pizza: press “Deliver” when the pizza is in the golden delivery zone. Three successful deliveries win. Calm mode offers manual positioning instead; VoiceOver and Reduce Motion enable manual control automatically.

Constellation: remember the numbered sequence. When the numbers disappear, tap the stars in the same order. “Show the order” offers another look. Three sequences complete the adventure.

Each completed game awards a fixed keepsake without duplicates. Completed adventures are saved; unfinished rounds restart after leaving. All three games can be replayed at any time.

All star chamber content remains: 18 animated keepsakes, the daily sphere journey, music and Cosmic Lost & Found. Three different keepsakes from games or daily journeys unlock the bonus room. Collected keepsakes can replay their animations.

Sayings, questions, favorites and sharing are now in the secondary “Sayings & silliness” area. They are written entertainment, not real predictions.

Choose your language in the menu on the main screen. Use the daily oracle or enter a question; all answers are fictional entertainment. Enter the star chamber and touch the large ball for a daily journey. Completed journeys lock until the next local calendar day; unfinished journeys can be resumed. The ordinary oracle stays open. Enable music with the speaker button; check device volume and silent mode. Music stops outside the room or in the background. Keepsakes are in the room; favorite sayings are behind the bookmark. Swipe to delete a favorite, or use About & privacy for local data deletion; the daily lock remains. No account or server recovery.
