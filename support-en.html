Crystal & Chaos — Help · Contact: mtierlusch@web.de · Provider: Gerrit Marc Lader

Choose your language in the menu on the main screen. Use the daily oracle or enter a question; all answers are fictional entertainment. Enter the star chamber and touch the large ball for a daily journey. Completed journeys lock until the next local calendar day; unfinished journeys can be resumed. The ordinary oracle stays open. Enable music with the speaker button; check device volume and silent mode. Music stops outside the room or in the background. Keepsakes are in the room; favorite sayings are behind the bookmark. Swipe to delete a favorite, or use About & privacy for local data deletion; the daily lock remains. No account or server recovery. For help, email app/iOS version, device and a short issue description to mtierlusch@web.de, without sensitive questions or favorites.
