Crystal & Chaos — Privacy notice

Provider/controller: Gerrit Marc Lader. Privacy contact: mtierlusch@web.de. Updated: 24.10.2026.

The app works offline and requires no account. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking and does not transmit your questions, favorites or usage data to us. Music and visual worlds are bundled, not downloaded from a server.

Your device stores your language, saved sayings and associated questions, music preference, daily journey start/completion status and collected keepsakes. These are used only for the corresponding features. Unsaved questions remain in memory during use. There is no app-operated cloud sync. Operating-system backups may include local app data according to your device settings.

Delete individual favorites or use About & privacy to remove favorites, keepsakes, music and language preferences. The minimal local daily lock is retained. Uninstalling removes local app data; restoring a device backup may restore it. When sharing, you choose the receiving service, whose privacy rules then apply. Apple may process its own diagnostics or store data according to your settings; the app integrates no such third-party SDK.

For questions or applicable privacy rights, contact mtierlusch@web.de. We receive no app user data and cannot remotely view or delete local contents.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub processes technical connection data such as your IP address to operate and secure the hosting service. We use no analytics, advertising services or cookies of our own. If you email us, we use your message and sender address to handle your request; please do not send sensitive app contents. Website and email processing is separate from the offline app.
